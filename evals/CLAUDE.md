# evals/ — working notes

`README.md` here is the reference for every metric, its `n` and its noise floor.
Read it before quoting or interpreting a number. `baselines.yaml` holds the
recorded values, so take numbers from there, not from memory or older docs.
The gold-set conventions (`null` means omitted, `expect.relevant` is derived) are
at the end of `README.md`.
